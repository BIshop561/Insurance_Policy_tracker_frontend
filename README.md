# Insurance_Policy_tracker_frontend
Insurance Policy Comparison and Renewal Tracker helps users compare insurance plans based on premiums, coverage, benefits, and exclusions. It tracks policy details, expiry dates, and renewal deadlines while sending timely reminders to prevent missed payments and policy lapses, ensuring efficient and hassle-free insurance management.

Key Features:

Policy Comparison: Compare insurance policies from different providers based on cost, coverage, benefits, and exclusions.
Renewal Tracking: Monitor policy expiry dates, renewal deadlines, and payment schedules.
Automated Reminders: Receive notifications for upcoming renewals and premium payments.
Policy Management: Store and organize policy details, documents, and insurer information in one place.
Premium Tracking: Track premium amounts, payment history, and renewal costs.
Dashboard and Reports: View active, expired, and upcoming policies through a user-friendly dashboard.
Objective: To simplify insurance policy selection and renewal management by providing a convenient, organized, and reliable solution that saves time, improves decision-making, and helps users maintain continuous insurance coverage.
